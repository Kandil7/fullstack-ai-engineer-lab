## 🎯 Success Metrics (Weekly)

- Minimum **1 commit per day** with meaningful artifact
- Minimum **1 Comet Thread** opened per working day
- Weekly Review completed every **Saturday**
- At least **1 new source** added to `learning-sources/` per week
- At least **1 module** progressed in `projects/`
